Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: Jackdaw does not collect any data about you. The developer has no servers and cannot see your packs, your answers or your progress. Everything stays on your device and, if you use iCloud sync on iPhone, in your own private iCloud.

Who is responsible

Jackdaw is developed by Orell Bühler, Switzerland. For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/OrellBuehler/jackdaw.

What the app stores on your device

  • The packs you import, with their content.
  • Your review history: which item you answered, when, and whether it was right. Your schedule and statistics are calculated from this.
  • Your settings, such as the daily goal, session size and theme.

This data is stored in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes it from that device.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or crash reporting tools. It does not contact any server run by the developer, because there is none.

iCloud sync (iPhone)

On iPhone, Jackdaw can sync through Apple's iCloud. Sync is on by default when you are signed in to iCloud and can be switched off at any time in the app under Settings, iCloud sync.

  • What is synced: your packs with their content, your review history, the list of packs you deleted, the daily goal and the session size.
  • Where it goes: the private database of your own iCloud account. Only you can read it. The developer has no access to it.
  • Who processes it: Apple, under the Apple Privacy Policy and your iCloud terms.
  • Notifications: the app uses silent push notifications from Apple so that a change on one device reaches your other devices. They contain no content and show nothing on screen.

To remove the synced data from iCloud, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (or Storage), choose Jackdaw and delete its data.

Online pack list (from version 1.1)

The sample library can load the current list of ready-made packs from GitHub. This happens only when you tap "Load list", and again when you add or update a pack from that list. The app then downloads public files from raw.githubusercontent.com. Nothing about you, your packs or your progress is sent. As with any download, GitHub receives your IP address and processes it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The packs that ship with the app work without this.

Android

The Android app keeps your packs and your progress on the device only. Up to version 1.0 it does not request the internet permission. From version 1.1 it requests it for one purpose: the online pack list described above.

Create with AI

The "Create with AI" feature copies a text prompt to your clipboard. Jackdaw itself does not contact any AI service. If you paste the prompt into a chatbot, what you type there is covered by that service's privacy policy, not this one.

App Store, Google Play and TestFlight

Apple and Google may collect information when you download or use apps from their stores, for example installation numbers or crash reports, if you agreed to share these with developers on your device. The developer only sees this in aggregated or anonymised form. If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple passes on your comment, screenshot, device details and the email address of your TestFlight account.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to operate the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Jackdaw collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to hand over, correct or delete on the developer's side. You control all of your data directly: on your device, and in your iCloud account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. The history of this page is public in the project repository.
