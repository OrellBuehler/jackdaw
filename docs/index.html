Features

Short sessions that add up

Ten minutes a day is enough. Jackdaw works out what is due, mixes in a few new items and tells you when you are done.

Spaced repetition

Every answer moves an item's next review. Easy things come back rarely, hard things come back soon.

Seven ways to be asked

Flashcards, multiple choice, typed answers, fill the gaps, true or false, ordering and open questions with a key point checklist.

Goals and streaks

Set a daily goal, keep your streak and watch accuracy and mastered items grow in the stats.

Practice without pressure

Drill a whole pack as a quiz, by typing or all mixed. Practice never changes your schedule.

iCloud sync

On iPhone, your packs and progress follow you to every device on your iCloud account. Reviews made offline are merged, not overwritten.

English and German

The app speaks both, in light and dark. Your packs can be in any language you like.

Open pack format

Your content, in a file you own

A pack is one plain JSON file. The format is open and documented, so anyone can write one: you, a script, or a chatbot.

  1. Tap Create with AI and copy the ready-made prompt.
  2. Tell any chatbot what you want to learn.
  3. Paste the answer back. Jackdaw checks it and shows what will be added.

Or start with the bundled sample library and add packs with one tap.

Nobody is watching you learn

Jackdaw has no servers. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing that reports back.

  • No account, no ads, no analytics
  • Everything is stored on your device
  • Sync goes to your own private iCloud, which only you can read
  • Open source, so you can check
